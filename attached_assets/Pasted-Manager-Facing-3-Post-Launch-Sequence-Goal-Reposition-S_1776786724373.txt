Rules for OpenClaw on the manager sequence:

Every CTA points to /teams. Never /signup, never /pricing. The individual free tier is irrelevant to this audience and dilutes the offer.
Always say "30-day free trial," never just "free trial." The 30 vs 14 distinction matters and operators will ask.
Always specify "Kirk reviews each request personally — 1 business day." This is the trust line; it's also true and removes the "is this a marketing funnel?" objection.
Lead with the operator's pain (turnover, inconsistency, bad reviews), never with the product. If the first sentence of any post is "ServeMaster Academy is a…", rewrite it.
Numbers in posts must be defensible. Use the pilot-cohort caveat any time a stat appears. If the stat can't be sourced, drop it — operators sniff out marketing math instantly and it kills credibility.
Post 2's screenshots need to be real. A mocked-up dashboard will be obvious to anyone who's looked at restaurant SaaS before. If real screenshots aren't available, use a clean abstracted illustration of the heatmap concept instead.
