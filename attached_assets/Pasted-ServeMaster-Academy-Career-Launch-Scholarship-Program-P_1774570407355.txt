ServeMaster Academy – Career Launch Scholarship Program
Program Overview
ServeMaster Academy is offering the Career Launch Scholarship — a limited opportunity for motivated restaurant servers to gain professional, job-ready skills that significantly improve their chances of landing better serving positions, earning higher tips, and building a stronger career in hospitality.
This scholarship provides full access to our structured AI-powered training platform, replacing inconsistent on-the-job learning with a repeatable, measurable system.
What Scholarship Recipients Receive

Full Individual Plan Access for 60 days (normally $19/month)
Complete access to all 30 training modules across 6 categories (Fine Dining, Beverage, Guest Experience, Menu & Upselling, Team Management, Tech)
150+ scenarios, including 36 structured AI role-play sessions at 3 difficulty levels
Voice Practice using Whisper-powered speech transcription
Full gamification: daily streaks, 12 badges, micro-challenges, and leaderboards
Trilingual support (English, French, Spanish)
Professional “ServeMaster Academy Certified Server” certificate with QR code upon successful completion

Program Requirements
To earn the certificate and be eligible for job placement support, participants must:

Complete 100% of the core training modules
Achieve a minimum 80% average on all quizzes
Complete at least 15 AI role-play scenarios
Provide a short testimonial or feedback at the end

Additional Opportunity for Top Graduates
Outstanding graduates will be added to our Job-Ready Graduate List. Restaurant managers and owners can review certified candidates, and I (Kirk Adamson, Founder) will personally facilitate introductions between strong graduates and restaurants looking to hire.
Why This Program Exists
Restaurant server training is broken — most new hires learn by watching, which is slow and inconsistent. The Career Launch Scholarship gives serious individuals the standardized knowledge, practical AI role-play practice, and voice confidence they need to stand out and succeed faster.
Limited Availability
Only 15 scholarships are available this month.
