Series Title: Covers
Website Description (Homepage / Series Page):
Luca Voss and Sofia Vale spent six years chasing excellence across the world’s greatest restaurants — from Paris and Tokyo to Buenos Aires and Toronto. They kept crossing paths in the most unexpected places, drawn to each other by something they couldn’t yet name.
What began as stolen glances slowly became something deeper. Along the way, they collected more than memories — they gathered the hard-won skills, instincts, and wisdom that would one day become ServeMaster Academy, the training platform they built together.
A cinematic slow-burn romance about passion, timing, and the love story behind the app.

Book 1: First Crossings
Website Description:
In the kitchens of Paris, Barcelona, and London, two servers notice each other again and again — but never quite connect. A charged, slow-burn romance begins with every near-miss.
Back of Book Blurb:
Luca Voss and Sofia Vale are both chasing the same thing: mastery.
As a young server and a rising chef, they move through the demanding world of fine dining — the heat of service, the precision of the floor, the unspoken language of great restaurants. Their paths cross three times across Europe: once in Paris, again in Barcelona, and finally in the rain-soaked streets of London.
Each time, something passes between them. A look. A spark. A question neither of them can answer yet.
But the timing is never right.
First Crossings is the beginning of a sweeping, six-year love story — and the origin of the real training platform, ServeMaster Academy, that would one day grow from everything they learned on the floor.

Book 2: Eastern Sparks
Website Description:
In Tokyo, Bangkok, and Singapore, Luca and Sofia finally speak — and the slow-burn romance ignites. But just as something real begins, life pulls them apart again.
Back of Book Blurb:
After years of near-misses, Luca Voss and Sofia Vale finally meet in Asia.
Working in some of the most demanding kitchens on the continent, they share late-night conversations, street food runs, and the quiet understanding that only comes from living the same relentless life. For the first time, they exchange numbers. For the first time, they stop pretending the pull between them is coincidence.
But contracts expire, visas end, and the world keeps moving.
Eastern Sparks deepens the romance while expanding the journey — and shows how the lessons they’re learning on the floor are quietly shaping something bigger than either of them yet realizes.

Book 3: Southern Flames
Website Description:
In Buenos Aires, Sydney, and Melbourne, the tension finally breaks. Luca and Sofia share their first real kiss — and begin to imagine building something lasting together.
Back of Book Blurb:
The fire has been lit.
In the passionate dining rooms of Buenos Aires and the precise, creative kitchens of Australia, Luca and Sofia stop running from what they feel. They exchange more than glances now — they exchange ideas, techniques, and the small, hard-won truths they’ve gathered from years on the floor.
For the first time, they talk about the future. About what it would mean to stop chasing opportunities on opposite sides of the world. About the knowledge they’ve collected and what it could become.
But family, visas, and the relentless demands of the industry still stand in their way.
Southern Flames is the emotional turning point of the series — where romance deepens and the seeds of ServeMaster Academy are first planted.

Book 4: The Table We Built
Website Description:
In Toronto, Luca and Sofia finally stop missing each other. Working side by side, they build the future they’ve been moving toward for six years — both in love and in the creation of ServeMaster Academy.
Back of Book Blurb:
They’ve traveled the world. They’ve mastered their craft. Now they’re finally in the same city, the same restaurant, and the same life.
In Toronto’s intense fine-dining scene, Luca Voss and Sofia Vale work together for the first time. The skills they spent years collecting across four continents are tested every service. But in their off-hours, they begin building something new — a training platform born from everything they’ve learned about great service, human connection, and the quiet moments that make the job worth doing.
As their romance finally finds solid ground, they create the one thing that could change the industry forever.
The Table We Built is the emotional and professional payoff of the Covers series — a love story that ends where ServeMaster Academy begins.
