A preview of what's inside

The full checklist has 15 items. Here are five to give you the idea.

1

New hire orientation complete before first floor shift

Restaurant values, house rules, and shift expectations reviewed in person — not just emailed.

2

Menu knowledge test passed before serving guests

Can they describe every dish, name the allergens, and suggest a wine pairing? Verify, don't assume.

3

Specials, features, and 86'd items briefed every shift

Consistency comes from a pre-shift ritual — not hoping they overheard something in the kitchen.

4

Guest complaint handling procedure reviewed

Every server should be able to handle a dissatisfied guest without escalating — and without losing the table.

5

Certification pathway communicated to all new hires

Servers who see a clear path to a certificate stay longer and perform better. Tell them on day one.
