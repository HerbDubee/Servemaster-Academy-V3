Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

The Free tier gives you permanent access to 3 modules and 5 scenarios — no time limit. It's a genuine free tier, not a trial. Upgrade to Pro whenever you're ready for the full experience.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay where supported. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

What's the difference between monthly and annual?

Annual billing works out to $12.42/month (vs $19/month) — you save 34% by paying upfront for a full year. Features are identical either way.

Can my whole restaurant team use ServeMaster?

Yes — each team member creates their own account. Restaurant managers can create a team and track everyone's progress from the Manager Dashboard (included in Pro).
