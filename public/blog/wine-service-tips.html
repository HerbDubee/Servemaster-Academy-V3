Wine Service

How to Sell Wine Confidently Without Being Pushy

The language and technique that lets you recommend wine naturally, increase check averages, and leave guests feeling well looked after — not sold to.

ServeMaster Academy · 7 min read

As a lifelong fine-dining aficionado, I've sat at hundreds of tables where the server clearly wanted to recommend a great bottle but hesitated. The result? Missed opportunities for both the guest and the restaurant.

In ServeMaster Academy, I dedicated an entire module to this exact skill. Here are the 5 techniques that actually work:

  1. Ask one simple question first: "Do you prefer something bold or elegant tonight?"
  2. Use the menu as your ally: Point to 2–3 options and explain why they pair with the dish.
  3. Tell a short story: "This Chianti is from the same vineyard that supplied the owner's wedding."
  4. Never say "expensive" — say "investment-worthy".
  5. Always offer a taste when possible.

Teams using this module see 25–35% higher wine sales within weeks.
