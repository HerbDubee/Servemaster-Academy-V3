The Wine Recommendation

Difficulty: Medium
G

"We're celebrating our anniversary — can you recommend a bottle of red? We usually like something smooth, not too tannic."

"Congratulations on your anniversary! For something smooth and approachable, I'd love to suggest our Malbec from Mendoza — it has rich dark fruit with a velvety finish, and pairs beautifully with the duck and the ribeye tonight."

Y
G

"That sounds lovely. What's the price point?"

✓ Coaching Feedback

Strong: You acknowledged the occasion immediately — guests remember that. Great food pairing mention with the duck and ribeye.

To improve: Lead with the guest's experience before the price. Try: "It's $68, and many of our guests say it's the highlight of their evening…" — price lands better after the emotion.
