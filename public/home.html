AI Guest · uncertain couple

"We're not really wine people honestly. Something not too dry, not too sweet? We were eyeing the Malbec but have no idea if that's even the right call."

You (as server)

"The Malbec is a great instinct — it's smooth, almost velvety, medium-bodied. If you're both doing the ribeye it'll pair beautifully. If you want something lighter, our Côtes du Rhône is also very approachable."

S
🍷

AI Guest · won over

She nudges her partner. "Ooh, velvety sounds good. We're both doing the ribeye actually — yeah, the Malbec. Let's do it."

✓ AI Coach Feedback Natural upsell

You validated their instinct first, used sensory language ("velvety"), and anchored the recommendation to their food order. Offering an alternative gave them agency without overwhelming. The sale closed itself.
