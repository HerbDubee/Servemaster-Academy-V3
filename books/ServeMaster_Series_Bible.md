# ServeMaster Book Series Bible v2 (Refined 2026-05-09)

## Core Structure
- **4 novels** (82k–95k words each).
- **POV:** Alternating third-person limited (close third) — readers live in Sofia Vale’s and Luca Voss’s heads.
- **Timeline:** Tight 6-year arc (Jan 2024–Dec 2029), 100% real cities/restaurants/landmarks/markets/parks/theaters/culinary schools.
- **Tie to ServeMaster:** Skill-building arc culminates in app prototype (Book4 Toronto). Weave natural lessons (service fundamentals, recovery, upselling) via experiences.
- **Hard rules:** Real establishments only (accurate details). Consult/update Bible per chapter. No fabrication.
