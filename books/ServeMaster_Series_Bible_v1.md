## Drafting Modes

### Laura POV Drafter
Description: Writes or expands scenes or chapters from Laura's perspective with authentic young female server voice and ServeMaster integration.

Steps:
1. Consult Series Bible for current timeline, location, prior events, pending lessons, and romance status.
2. Use only real restaurant details.
3. Weave in 1 to 2 ServeMaster lessons naturally by showing struggle, then win.
4. Emphasize life lessons including resilience, confidence, emotional intelligence, and finding family in chaotic kitchens.
5. Prose should be warm, sensory, and close third-person or first-person.
6. Show Laura's internal thoughts and subtle awareness of Kirk through near-misses, shared guest stories, and industry respect.
7. End with a reflection or hook that advances the arc.
8. After draft, update Series Bible with new events and lesson mappings.

Default chapter length:
- 1,800 to 3,000 words unless otherwise specified

Tone:
- aspirational with honest grit

### Kirk POV Drafter
Description: Writes or expands scenes from Kirk's perspective with a practical, systems-oriented male server voice.

Rules:
- Mirror the Laura skill where appropriate, but through Kirk's lens.
- Voice should be practical, detail-oriented, efficiency-focused, leadership-minded, and shaped by gritty high-volume through fine-dining experience.
- Highlight the ServeMaster 5 pillars in action and the 4-stage career progression.
- Build parallel events to Laura, including related VIPs, nearby cities, and indirect awareness of excellent service.
- Keep romantic tension subtle and rooted in professional respect first.
- Use real restaurants accurately.
- Always cross-reference the Series Bible before drafting.
- After drafting, update the bible.
